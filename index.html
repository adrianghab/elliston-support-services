For NDIS participants

Support coordination

A small number of places available Get in touch

We currently have a small number of places available for Support Coordination for NDIS participants in the Elliston community.

Having a local Support Coordinator means you get support from someone who understands the local community, knows the services available in the region, and is familiar with the challenges that come with accessing supports in our part of the country.

We can help you understand and make the most of your NDIS plan, connect with suitable providers and community services, coordinate your supports, and work alongside you to build greater choice, confidence and independence.

Where possible, we focus on connecting you with local people and services, while also helping you access supports from further afield when they are needed.

If you or someone you know could benefit from local Support Coordination, get in touch to discuss your needs and availability.

Support work

Local people, local support

Get in touch

We believe good support starts with a strong connection to the local community.

We proudly employ local people from the Elliston community to provide domestic assistance, cleaning, personal support, community access and transport when needed.

Choosing local team members means you can have familiar faces you know and trust, who understand the local area and the community around you. It also means your support can be more flexible, personal and connected to everyday life in Elliston.

By supporting local workers, we’re also helping to build local employment and keep more support services within our community.

Allied health

Connecting You With Allied Health Support

Request a service

At Elliston Support Services, we believe accessing the right support should feel simple, local and personal. We can help connect you with trusted allied health professionals who can support you to live more independently, stay active and work towards the things that matter most to you.

We can help connect you with a range of local allied health professionals, including

  • Physiotherapists
  • Occupational therapists
  • Podiatrists
  • Psychologists
  • Speech Pathologists
  • Dieticians

We’re here to help you find the right support for your individual needs and circumstances.

If you think allied health support could be helpful for you or someone you support, feel free to get in touch and request a service. We’ll do our best to help you find the right person and make the process as easy as possible.

Local people, local support, connected to the right care.

Support workers

Want to work with us?

Get in touch

We’re always interested in hearing from local people who are passionate about helping others in their community.

Our support workers provide person-centred support including domestic assistance, community access, transport and personal care.

Whether you have previous experience in support work or simply have the right attitude and willingness to help, we’d love to hear from you.

Working locally means building genuine relationships with people in our community while enjoying the flexibility to take on support work that suits your availability and experience.

If you’re friendly, reliable and would like to work with us get in touch with us to find out about current opportunities.
